This application was born out of a desire for a high-functioning, independent task management tool. Dissatisfied with the 'software-as-a-service' (SaaS) paywalls of existing market options, I utilized my background in development to architect a private solution. The project transitioned from conceptual research to full-scale implementation to provide a seamless, self-hosted management experience.

The development process was structured across multiple environments—from initial prototyping to final deployment—to maintain high standards of quality. I utilized an Agile framework to manage tasks, ensuring that each phase of the project was executed with precision and adaptability.
